About me

Digging deeper into
soft robots ✦

I'm a PhD student at Princeton's Faboratory, advised by Prof. Rebecca Kramer-Bottiglio. I research burrowing robots — designing systems that mimic the compliance and adaptability of biological organisms. My work sits at the intersection of materials science, mechanical engineering, and embodied intelligence.

Outside the lab, I run a one-person ice cream kitchen, chasing bold flavor combinations with the same curiosity I bring to robot design.
